## Responsibilities of a Cyber security analyst

 A cyber security analyst is the person who monitors and protects an organization's systems, data and assets by identifying threats , finding vulnerabilities and helping reduce security risks.
 
Their responsibilities consists of :

* monitoring networks and systems
* investigating security incidents
* responding to incidents
* identifying vulnerabilities
* manage and recommend security controls
* analyze logs
* perform risk assessments
*  Help protect CIA triad
* improve security practices
* document incidents and findings

   For an entry security analysts they are not required to do all of this since they will gain experience as they are working.
   But most importantly they should be able to monitor systems , protect information and search for weaknesses.

  ## Ethics in Cyber Security

We already know that cybersecurity professionals have privileges like accessing sensitive data. They have to respect that privilege; it is crucial to adhere to established policies and protocols. Security professionals must also remain unbiased and uphold security and confidentiality even when personal relationships are involved.

#### Key ethical Principles

**Confidentiality**: professionals have an ethical duty to keep private information , such as PII and SPII confidential and secure. 

**Privacy protections**: Safeguarding personal information from unauthorized use is essential , and professionals should always follow company policies regarding data access and sharing. 

#### laws and compliance considerations 

security professionals must understand and comply with laws and regulations that govern data handling and security. negligence in following compliance regulations can lead to serious consequences , including legal repercussions and loss of data. 

---

### Deliberate PII handling 

PII is personally identifiable information, so organizations must be deliberate about how PII or SPII is handled , where it is stored and consistently aware of iits storage location.

**Consequences of Non-Compliance**

* Failure to meet PII obligations can lead to government regulators inspecting a company's data handling practices.
* Customers and businesses may directly inquire about data handling, impacting customer relationships, especially with sensitive data.
